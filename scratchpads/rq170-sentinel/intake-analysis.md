# RQ-170 Sentinel: intake analysis

Sources: `ref/three-view.png` (orthographic top, side, front; 640 x 344, the geometry source) and
`ref/render-sheet.png` (three CG renders; used only to settle what the drawing hides).

## Observations (measured, top view, 1 px = 0.03328 m at an assumed 20 m span)

- Span 601 px, nose to wingtip trailing edge 224 px: span-to-length 2.68.
- Leading edge straight from the apex to 9.6 m at 0.638 aft per metre of span (32.5 degrees from lateral); the last 0.4 m is a rounded raked tip ending at 7.05 m aft.
- Trailing edge kinked: centre at 6.26 m aft, moving forward to 5.49 m at 3.3 m span, then aft at 0.358 per metre to 7.53 m at 9.0 m.
- Front view: upper wing surface flat; thickness 0.2 m at the tip, 0.33 m at 7.3 m, 0.53 m at 4 m, 0.8 m at 2 m, all growth on the underside. Centre depth 1.4 to 1.7 m including the hump.
- Hump 0.5 m above the wing on the centreline; two lateral bumps 0.4 m high at 1.67 m span.
- Side view: hump peak at 30 to 37 percent of length; the side view's own length is 15 percent short of the top view, so it is used for ratios only.
- Wheels all touch one ground line 1.86 m below the wing upper surface; main track 1.7 to 2.0 m.

## Inferences (labelled, not measured)

- Nose is the planform apex; the dark oval behind it is the dorsal intake, so the hump's steep face is forward and the exhaust is at the centre trailing edge. The render sheet's exhaust box supports the aft slot.
- Sensor blisters taper aft (drawing); the render sheet is ambiguous.
- Nose gear at 2.0 m aft from the render sheet; the drawing's side view puts it under the apex, which is not believed.
- Main gear carries tandem twin wheels (render sheet side view).
- Dorsal surface behind the hump and everything the underside hides are unobserved.

## Materials

Single matte light grey composite skin; near-black tyres and duct interiors; pale grey painted alloy gear. Roundels and small vents are albedo-only markings.
