Gait
Stride is capped by the leg workspace (0.62 m forward / 0.46 m back from the hip at reach 1), so raising Stride past ~1.1 does nothing until Leg reach goes up too.
Natural locomotion
The foot scheduler only decides where feet land. This layer derives a stride phase from those footfalls and drives everything above the ankles from it. Movement dynamics is what bots run with (turn lag, head turn, bob/sway) — leave it on to match them.
Spine falloff spreads the twist/lean up the body: 1 is linear by height, above 1 keeps it in the chest, below 1 pushes it down into the waist.
