Click a bone to select it. Shift-click to select the whole chain it belongs to. Clicking a chain you have already started adds the rest of it rather than removing what you have.

Pose

How far up 4 bones
—

Dragging a bone bends this many bones above it. At zero it bends every bone up to the root. If you have selected bones above the one you grab, those are bent instead.

Turning Pose on stops the clip. Turning it off lets the clip take the bones back, so save the pose first if you want to keep it.
