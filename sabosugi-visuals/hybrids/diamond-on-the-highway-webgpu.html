WebGPU/TSL port of diamond-on-the-highway.html. This one follows convert-then-merge: "Xenolith Diamond" and "Highway to Heaven" are each ported to their own standalone wgslFn (own uniforms, own helper fns, independently callable), then composed in one line of TSL (select on the diamond's hit flag) instead of hand-fusing one giant WGSL block. Drag/scroll orbits+zooms the diamond; the tunnel keeps flying regardless.
