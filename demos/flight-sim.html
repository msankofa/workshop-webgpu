0.00

Rain thickens the haze, greys the sky and cloud deck, dims the sun and wets the ground. Above 0.3 it storms: bolts from the cloud base to the ground, with thunder arriving at the speed of sound.

Opponents

Unarmed aircraft are painted orange, drawn dashed on the HUD, and never shoot, lock or hunt. Nothing else attacks them either, so they stay yours to practise on. Solo and Training also switch the ground sites off.

0.35

Assist bends your rounds toward the lead solution for whatever is nearest your boresight, and falls off with angle, so it helps a near miss and does nothing for a wild one. 0 is pure iron sights.

0.50

Every craft-sized explosion throws fragments that bounce and flicker out, and wreckage that tumbles, smoulders and trails dust for about twenty seconds. Momentum is how much of the dead aircraft's speed the pieces launch with: at 0 the wreckage stops dead in the air, at 1 it keeps flying. Nothing in the sim slows it down afterwards, so the inherited speed is capped at 90 m/s and a wreck's downward half is left to the ground.
