SDF Pikachu

Spike: is a Stadium model drawable as a distance field?

Joint blend is the smooth union, and it starts at zero. It exists to close the seam a rotated bone tears open in a rigid-skinned model, which nothing on this page does — so here it only melts the creature. Measured on Pikachu, a blend of 0.05 puts the surface 1.31% of body height off the real mesh where zero puts it 0.13%.
